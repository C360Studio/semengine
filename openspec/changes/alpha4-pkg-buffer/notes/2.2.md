## Choices the design leaves open

- **Waiting for space (R3, R4).** The `notFull` condition variable is replaced by a channel, `spaceFreed`,
  which a waiting writer creates under the lock. `Read`, `ReadBatch`, `Clear` and `Close` close it and clear the
  field, which wakes every waiting writer to check again. `Write` under Block waits on it alone. The bounded
  writes share one helper, `writeBounded`, that waits in a single `select` on the space channel, the context's
  `Done` channel (`WriteWithContext`) or a `time.Timer` (`WriteWithTimeout`). No goroutine is started and no
  context is created. `ReadBatch` used to signal once per item freed; now it wakes all waiters once.
- **Already-ended bounds (D1).** The closed check comes first, then a bound that has already ended refuses
  before any wait, as at the pin. A timeout of zero or less uses a package-level closed channel as the bound.
- **R5.** The nil check is in `WriteWithContext` before the policy dispatch, so it refuses under every policy:
  `errs.WrapInvalid(errNilContext, "Buffer", "WriteWithContext", "nil context")`.
- **R6.** `Write` and `Clear` do their work in `write` and `clear`, which hold the lock and return the dropped
  items; the callback is called after they return, so after the unlock, at all three sites.
- **Shared code.** `push` (add an item and record it) and `recordDrop` are taken out of `Write`, so
  `writeBounded` uses the same write path.
- **R7.** The five pin tests are rewritten inside `synctest.Test`. They use `synctest.Wait` instead of sleeps.
  Goroutine counts come from the bubble's stack (`bubbleGoroutinesIn`) instead of `runtime.NumGoroutine`.
  `TestBlockingPolicyWithTimeout` drops its elapsed bound; `TestWriteWithTimeoutExpires` pins the timing on the
  fake clock. Calls go through `Buffer[T]` (R11) rather than a type assertion. `TestConcurrentContextCancellations`
  (`buffer_test.go:591`, not in R7's list) is left as at the pin: its 50 ms context deadline is the behaviour
  under test, and the test sleeps nowhere.
- **README.** `task verify` runs `docs:check`, and the pin's `README.md` failed markdownlint. This task makes only
  formatting fixes (`markdownlint-cli2 --fix`, plus one wrapped line). Content edits are task 3.2 (R8).

## Defect in ported surface, fixed here

`NewCircularBuffer` returned `newCircularBuffer`'s `(*circularBuffer[T], error)` as `(Buffer[T], error)`. On a
metrics-registration error the returned `Buffer[T]` was therefore a non-nil interface holding a nil pointer, so
`buf != nil` reported a buffer. It now returns a nil `Buffer[T]` with its error (`buffer.go`, `NewCircularBuffer`).
This is a defect in ported surface that the task fixes, by the coordinator's direction; it changes no design row.
Failing first, `go test -count=1 -run TestNewCircularBufferRefusedReturnsNil ./pkg/buffer/`:

```text

    metrics_test.go:108: NewCircularBuffer returned a non-nil Buffer[int] (*buffer.circularBuffer[int]) with its error
FAIL
```
