# Task 3.4: T2, Close is not held by a held Subscribe

Choices the design ("Tests that replace the probes", T2) does not spell out.

- **T2 reuses T1's set-up** (`newHeldClient`, `holdPublish`), then starts a `Subscribe` in its own goroutine.
- **"Admitted" is read as the count of running subscriptions**, `client.running[workSubscription] > 0`, which
  `admitLocked` raises under natsclient's lock. The test reads it with `TryRLock` inside `probe.Await`: a `Subscribe`
  that keeps the lock while it waits for nats.go makes every observation fail with "natsclient's lock is held", and the
  wait ends at `lifecycleBound` (10 s) instead of hanging.
- **What T2 asserts after `Close`.** As T1: `Close` returns an error matching `context.DeadlineExceeded` within 10 s, the
  held publish returns an error, and the connection reaches `CLOSED` (`requireClosed`). And the spec's scenario "A held
  call does not hold Close": the `Subscribe` returns an error. T2 does not require a particular error. Which one comes
  back depends on what takes nats.go's lock first once the cut has released it: the check task 3.5 moves out of the
  lock, the round-trip check after the `SUB`, or nats.go's own `subscribe` refusing a draining connection
  (`nats.ErrConnectionDraining`, returned unwrapped by `subscribeOwned`). Superseded at stop 2: that refusal now
  returns `nats.ErrConnectionClosed` once `Close` has begun, and T2 requires it (notes/3.5.md, "Stop-2 review: a
  native subscribe refused during Close").
