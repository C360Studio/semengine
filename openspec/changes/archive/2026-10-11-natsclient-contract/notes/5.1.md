Each row of `cleanup.md` assigned to 5.1, where it landed (the first line of the edited text, on this branch at

## `doc.go` (`d77c029`)

- **M47** `doc.go:4`. "Exponential backoff for reconnection" and "context propagation throughout" are gone. It now
  says what a context ends (the wait for the server), what it does not (a wait behind a held write, P13, P14), and
  that `Close`, on a connection the client dialled, and `Connect` are the exception (T1, T4; ADR-112). Line 2's
  "automatic reconnection" stays: natsclient keeps nats.go's reconnect (ADR-112 §5).
- **M50** `doc.go:79`. The cluster premise is marked not measured, since no test here runs a cluster; "is decided by
  cluster timing" became "can be".
- **M51** `doc.go:87`. The consumer setups' re-observation is natsclient's own logic, cited to
  `stream_visibility_test.go` and named as a stand-in test; the cluster window it waits out is marked as M50.
- **M52** `doc.go:115`. "MaxBytes 0 and -1 both mean unlimited to JetStream (P16)". The MaxAge `-1` claim is gone. The
  requirement of a finite MaxAge stays, because `CheckStreamBounds` refuses `MaxAge <= 0`
  (`stream_bounds.go:88`), which is natsclient's own rule. MaxAge 0's meaning is not stated, as the row gives none.
- **M57** `doc.go:361`. natsclient asks nats.go to wait `ReconnectWait` (2 s by default, `WithReconnectWait`). How
  nats.go paces the attempts is marked not promised, as ADR-112 §5 does. The one-minute exponential backoff is the
  circuit breaker's: while the circuit is open, `Connect` (`client.go:747-750`) and other calls return
  `ErrCircuitOpen`.
- **M59** `doc.go:460`. As M42: every call that waits for the server takes a context, which ends that wait. A held
  write is not ended (P13), and `Close` and `Connect` are the exception (T1, T4; P18). `RTT` takes no context, which
  is its signature, so it cites nothing.
- **5.2, `doc.go` half** `doc.go:300-301`. `WithWriteTimeout` ("default: one minute, nats.go's") and
  `WithPingInterval` ("default: 30 s"), aligned with the entries around them.
