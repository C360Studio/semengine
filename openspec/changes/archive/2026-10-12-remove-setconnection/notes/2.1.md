# Task 2.1 notes

- `buildTableClient` no longer registers `t.Cleanup(dialled.Close)`. Without `SetConnection` the connection Connect
  dialled stays the client's until Close, so the client's own Close closes it: in the closing state the test's first
  Close (`tc.firstClose`), in the others `closeInCleanup`'s Close or the cell's own `CloseLive` or `CloseEnded`.
  Close drains the connection and closes it when the drain ends or times out (`drainAndCloseConnection`), and cuts its
  socket when its context ends (`cleanup`'s `cutWhenDone`), so no cell leaves the connection open.
- The local `dialled` stays: the closing state still publishes on it and listens for its drain.
- With the recording handler gone, `buildTableClient` builds its client with the default logger. No cell read the
  log except the deleted `SetConnection` warn check.
