# 3.6 Failure outcomes in the method docs

Design D7. `Create`, `Append` and `Reconcile` say a request that is incomplete or outside the contract is refused
before it is sent (`MutationInvalid`, not committed), that any other failure follows `ClassifyMutationError`, and that
a nil error means the commit is verified. `Delete` says the same for its zero `ExpectedRevision`. `Reconcile` adds
that it reads first: a failed read sends no mutation and is not committed with operation `read-authoritative`, and a
stored entity outside the contract is refused as `MutationInvalid`. `ReadAuthoritative` says every failure is not
committed. Gate: review.
