# Task 3.6 notes

Choices the design does not spell out:

- A child left out (absent or not lifecycle-managed) is left out quietly, as `List` leaves one out; the Warn the old
  loop logged for every skipped child goes. A linked child that is gone is ordinary eventual graph state, not a
  degrade, and every other read failure now fails the call.
- The error names the parent and the child and wraps the read error, so `errors.Is` reaches `nats.ErrTimeout` and
  `ErrWorkflowNotRegistered`.
- `ChildSpec.Workflow` and `ChildOptions.Limit`/`Offset` docs now say what Children does (they promised the
  skip-with-Warn behaviour).
- `TestIntegration_ChildrenLoadsLinkedChildrenAcrossWorkflows` is re-aimed: its Warn assertions go, the orphan link
  into an unregistered workflow moves to a last subtest that asserts `ErrWorkflowNotRegistered`, and a new case
  ("a page past the gone link", subtask Limit 2) shows a page filled past a dropped link.
