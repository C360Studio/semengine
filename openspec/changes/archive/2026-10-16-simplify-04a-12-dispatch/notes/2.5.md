# Task 2.5 notes

`internal/dispatch/keyed_pool_test.go` runs on the bubble clock (design D6). The rewrite landed with task 2.4's
signature changes in commit `6155380d60736e6f1e2ab3ee78ba65671fada1d6` (`notes/2.4.md`, "2.5 rides the same edit").

## What changed

- Every test that waits runs in `synctest.Test`. The `time.After` watchdogs, the 100 ms submit deadline and the 5 s
  `stopCtx` are gone; `shutdown(t, p)` shuts a pool down under an hour's deadline on the bubble clock, which never
  elapses unless every goroutine in the bubble is blocked. A wait the pool should end but does not fails as a
  bubble deadlock.
- `TestKeyedPool_ProcessUsesConstructorContext` drops its `context.WithValue`; the identity check (`got != runCtx`)
  proves `Process` received the exact constructor context.
- `TestKeyedPool_DifferentKeysConcurrent` waits with `synctest.Wait()` and requires all four items to be in `Process`
  at once, in place of a two-second wait per item.
- `TestKeyedPool_Backpressure` submits a third item on the full lane from a goroutine, waits with `synctest.Wait()`,
  requires that the submit has not returned, then cancels its context and requires `context.Canceled`. It also
  requires that only `first` and `second` reached `Process`.

## Choices the design does not spell out

- **Lane-written state without locks.** Where only one lane writes a slice or counter and the test reads it after
  `Shutdown` has joined the lanes, the test drops its mutex (`SameKeyOrdered`, `PanicRecovery`); `SameKeySameLane`
  keeps its mutex because eight lanes could write its map if routing were wrong. Atomics become `atomic.Int64`.
- **`ShutdownSubmitRace` in a bubble.** Its submitters spin without blocking, so the bubble's clock does not advance
  while they run; the race between submits and `Shutdown` is unchanged.
