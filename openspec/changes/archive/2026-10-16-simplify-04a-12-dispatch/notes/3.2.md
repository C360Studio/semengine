## Choices the design does not spell out

- **The Debug line** keeps the lane beside the entity ID (the pool's line had pool and lane), and logs the error with
  `slog.Any`, as `processIngest`'s own lines do. Its message names `processIngest`, the function whose error it is.
- **`Process` documents that the pool reports nothing about the outcome**, since the error it used to log is gone:
  `Process` disposes of its own failures.
