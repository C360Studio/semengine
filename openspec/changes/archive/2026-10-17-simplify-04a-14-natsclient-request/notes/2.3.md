## What moved

- `MaxRetries: n` became `MaxAttempts: n+1` everywhere: `RoundTripPreservesClass` 2 → 3, `RetriesThenSucceeds`
  10 → 11 (its comment now says eleven attempts, ten retries, before jitter), the cancel-in-flight test 2 → 3,
  `ErrorReplyIsNotRetried` 2 → 3, the no-responder test 3 → 4.
- `DefaultRetryConfig()` became `retry.DefaultConfig()`. Local variables named `retry` are now `cfg`, since `retry`
  is the package.
- Rows for `Request` and `RequestWithRetry` are dropped from `TestExportedNilContextRefusedOnRequestAndBucketPaths`,
  `TestReviewerExportedNATSNilContextRefuses`, `TestRetryRequestEndedContextNeverDispatches` and
  `TestRequestCancelledInFlightRecordsNoFailure`. The positive control in `TestReviewerExportedNATSNilContextRefuses`
  calls `RequestClassified`.
- `TestIntegration_Request`, `_Timeout`, `_DefaultTimeout`, `_NotConnected`, `TestReconnectAfterConnectContextEnded`
  and the two `SubscribeForRequests` tests call `RequestClassified`; names unchanged.
- Deleted: `TestIntegration_LegacyRequest_SuccessBodyUnchanged`, `TestIntegration_RequestWithRetry_Success`.

- `TestIntegration_RequestWithRetry_NoResponder` is renamed `TestIntegration_RequestWithRetryClassified_NoResponder`,
