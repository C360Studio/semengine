# Task 4.1 notes

## Choices the design does not spell out

- The table test is `TestIndexStatusResponseWire` in `graph/index_status_test.go` (package `graph`). It replaces
  `TestIndexStatusResponse_FailedDetailWireRoundTrip`, `_StalenessWireRoundTrip` and `_BootstrapCompleteWireRoundTrip`
  (`graph/readiness/index_status_test.go`, deleted), `TestBootstrapScope_WireRoundTrip`
  (`graph/readiness/backlog_status_test.go`) and `TestIndexStatusResponseHasNoLegacyFields`
  (`graph/write_rules_test.go`). The `// Requirement: graph-transport-boundary/...` line moves with it.
- Each case decodes a wire value, compares the decoded envelope, encodes it again and checks the keys present (with
  their JSON values) and absent. A case naming an envelope starts from the envelope's own encoding, so it is also a
  round trip. The cases are the old tests' assertions, one per row: failure detail present and absent, staleness
  present and absent, `bootstrap_complete` false, true and missing, `bootstrap_scope` set, zero and missing on a
  complete envelope.
- The legacy-field check changes from reading struct tags with `reflect` to decoding a wire value that carries
  `phase`, `revision` and `last_synced` and checking none of them comes back out. A field added for any of them, with
  or without `omitempty`, is decoded from the wire and fails the decode comparison. `published_at` and
  `indexed_revision` are checked present on the re-encoding.
- `computeBase`, used only by `backlog_status_test.go`, moves there.
