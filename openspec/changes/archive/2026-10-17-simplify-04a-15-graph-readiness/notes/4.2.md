## Choices the design does not spell out

- The package comment stays in `watcher.go`. It states ADR-109 Decision 3 in three parts: the bucket is information
  for operators and tools, the gate's one SemEngine reader will be graph-index asking in process, and the Watcher is
  kept for the pin's readers (fusion, graph-clustering) when they are ported. The request/reply history it carried is
  cut to the reason the Watcher exists (a read needs no NATS round trip). The `#110` pointer for `published_at` goes;
  the comment says only that the Watcher does not read it.
- Beyond the five statements D6 lists, three more said the retired authoritative-absence license (ADR-084) still
  held, and are cut: `watcher.go`'s commit-time comparison ("the envelope's authoritative-absence license makes it
  expensive"), `index_status.go`'s `BacklogStatusInputs` ("the one projection whose output gates
  authoritative-absence claims"), and `readiness_gate.go`'s package-level history of the gate's modes.
- `readiness_gate.go`'s producer invariant keeps its rule and why; the reason a new producer keeps it now names the
  revision comparison a caller makes and `ComputeBacklogStatus`, in place of graph-index's `latchBootstrap` and
  responder gate, which are not ported.
- Comments on how nats.go or the standard library behave keep their wording: the watch's delivery of the current
  value on bind and the end-of-initial-values marker (`watcher.go`), the consumer-clock skew sentence, the
  `AckFloor.Stream` measurement (`index_status.go`), and `time.Ticker` dropping missed ticks (`publisher.go`). In the
  commit-time paragraph only the sentence after the delivery claim changes.
- `gh#NNN` reads `SemStreams gh#NNN` in production and test comments; unported paths read `pin:`. The Prometheus
  `Help` text of `bootstrap_complete` still says `gh#474`: it is exposed metric text, not a comment, and D6 changes
  comments only.
- `KeyGraphIngest`'s comment named `graph.ComputeBacklogStatus`; the function is `readiness.ComputeBacklogStatus`
  here, so it now reads `ComputeBacklogStatus`. graph-ingest's `statusMetricsInterval` comment named the unexported
  multiplier; it now says three heartbeats.
- The ledger row's contract adds (6)–(9) for D1–D5; `known_risks` drops #135 and keeps `Reading.Raw` for fusion's
  port (#110); `proving_tests` drops `bucket_alias_test.go` and `index_status_test.go` (removed in 4.1) and names
  `graph/index_status_test.go`.
