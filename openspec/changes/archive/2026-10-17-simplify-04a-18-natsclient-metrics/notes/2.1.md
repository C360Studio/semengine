## Choices the design does not spell out

- The scripted server's `INFO` now offers `"headers":true`. `Client.Publish` injects trace headers, and nats.go
  refuses a header publish to a server that does not offer them (`nats: headers not supported by this server`, seen
  on the first run of the publish row). Real servers since 2.2 offer headers. No other scripted test publishes.
- The publish row sends its PUB through `Client.Publish`, then a nats.go flush on `GetConnection()` so the server
  reads a PING to withhold. That keeps the design's four steps (wait on `pinged`, write `-ERR`, `answerPings`,
  check) for the publish row too.
- The count is read inside the handler's `Handle` by gathering the registry and reading the series by its
  Prometheus name and `reason` label, not through the client's collector, so the oracle is independent of the
  implementation's field.
- The waits use the package's `await`/`awaitValue`/`awaitErr` helpers (bounded by `lifecycleBound`); there is no
  sleep and no synctest bubble.
