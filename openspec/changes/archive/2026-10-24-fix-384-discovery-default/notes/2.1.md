# Notes on task 2.1

Base: `0a594d7` (the design commit), go1.26.10 darwin/arm64.

## Assertion lines

Task 2.2's mutants name these lines with `-expect`:

- `config/load_test.go:331`: the key-set assertion in `TestLoad_DefaultServicesAreHeartbeatOnly`, the sorted
  service keys against the literal `[]string{"heartbeat"}`. M1 and M2 expect this line.
- `config/load_test.go:332`: the `Enabled` assertion, `cfg.Services["heartbeat"].Enabled` is true. M3 expects this
  line.
- `config/config_test.go:251`: the example-config key-set assertion in `TestLoader_ExampleConfig`, the sorted service
  keys against the literal `[]string{"heartbeat", "message-logger"}`.

## Choices the design leaves open

- The new test sits after `TestLoad_RefusesNullDocument` in `config/load_test.go`, among the other loader-wide tests.
- In `TestLoader_ExampleConfig` the `message-logger` `Enabled` check stays; only the two discovery lines go, and the
  key-set assertion takes the place of the second.
- `config_test.go` now imports `maps` and `slices` for the key-set assertion.
