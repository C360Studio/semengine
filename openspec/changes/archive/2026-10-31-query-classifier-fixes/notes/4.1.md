## Choices the design leaves open

- Both tests go through `NewClassifierChain(NewKeywordClassifier(), nil).ClassifyQuery` and compare the whole
  `Options` map, so a stray extra hint fails the row as well as a wrong field. A descriptive mention must give exactly
  the default result (tier 0, empty options, confidence 1.0).
- The expected values are written by hand from #94's table and design D1; the fresh cases use field words no list
  in the code holds (`flow_rate`, `kilowatt_hours`, `turbidity`), so a field list would fail them.
- "What is the average of?" keeps avg with no field: once `of` is skipped nothing follows, and inventing `of` as the
  field is the defect.
- An extremum whose field word is `of` or `the` ("the lowest of values") is not an extremum: D1 asks for one field
  word, and those two are the words the binding skips. Not tested by name; it follows the same skip list.
- The clause rule (`which`, `who`, `with` only in the extremum's own clause) is shown both ways: "Which pump failed;
  give the highest pressure reading" and "List pumps with alarms but give the lowest pressure reading" bind, and the
  three descriptive mentions of design § Proving tests do not. D2 reuses the same clause function.
- Generated checks: not used. The inputs are word sequences with one stated rule each (skip `of`/`the`; which/who/with
  in the clause); the named cases and their controls cover each rule both ways. `FuzzKeywordClassify` (task 5.2)
  checks the no-panic law over the same code.
