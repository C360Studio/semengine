## Choices the design leaves open

- `NewNATSCommunityStorage(kv *natsclient.KVStore) (*NATSCommunityStorage, error)` refuses a nil store with an invalid
  error rather than panicking at first use. The design names the constructor, not its signature.
- `GetAllCommunities` returns records in key order, and fails, naming the key, when an entry's own level and ID do not
  give its key back (`{}` or another group's record), as well as on a read or decode error. A key removed between the
  listing and its read fails the call too (the spec's "cannot be read"); the caller retries.
- Unexported storage surface for the maintainer (task 3.3): `save`, `remove(ctx, key)` (a missing key is already
  removed), `keys` (sorted), `communityKey`. `save` and `remove` validate the key with
  `natsclient.ValidateKVLiteralKey` before writing.
- `TestGetAllReportsCancel` drives a pre-cancelled context through the pinned nats.go against a real server: the
  listing returns `context.Canceled`, so no extra check after the listing was added.
- `extractWords` scans bytes: ASCII letters fold to lower case, every other byte (including all non-ASCII) separates
  words. The pin lower-cased with `strings.ToLower` first, which folds a few non-ASCII letters (the Kelvin sign) into
  ASCII; the byte scan is the design's definition of a word, and allocates nothing for a long literal.
- `entityWords` keeps the pin's skip of relationship triples (`Triple.IsRelationship`): their object is another
  entity's ID, whose positions are not words.
- `newCommunity` (in `terms.go`) builds a record from a group's counts; the maintainer (3.3) owns the counts.
  `TypeCounts` keeps the `MaxKeywords` most common types; the type is the ID's fifth position.
- `graph/clustering/doc.go` holds a two-line package comment so `task lint` passes (revive `package-comments`); task
  4.3 replaces it.
