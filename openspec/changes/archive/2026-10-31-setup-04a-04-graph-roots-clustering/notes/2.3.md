# Task 2.3: the generated checks of design D5

`TestMaintainerMatchesModel` and `TestHistoryOrderIrrelevant` (`graph/clustering/maintainer_prop_test.go`), Rapid
`v1.3.0`. The reference model is written in the test from the spec and design D2: it splits IDs on `.` itself, takes
each entity's words from the generated word list (the value's text is that list joined by spaces, every word valid by
construction), applies the `MaxEntityTerms` cap with lexical ties, builds the summary from the template text, and
never calls `terms.go` or `maintainer.go`.

## What makes each assertion run

- `TestMaintainerMatchesModel` compares every record and `Tick`'s result with the model after every tick past
  `BootstrapDone`, and checks `Upsert`'s and `Delete`'s errors at every step. A fixed prefix in every history upserts an
  entity with 40 distinct words (the cap applies) and five members with words in one source (its level-1 and level-2
  records hold `MaxRepEntities` representatives). A fixed suffix upserts an entity no generated delivery names, ticks,
  deletes it and ticks, so three groups become empty and their removal is compared in every check. The bucket starts
  with six records an earlier run left (three of an entity alone in groups whose keys sort first, which the suffix
  deletes before the last good tick); none is in the model. Generated operations add failing ticks: the listing, a write
  or a removal fails after 0 to 3 bucket operations, nothing is compared then, and the next good tick must match the
  model (review of `42ad8eb`, MEDIUM 1). Other generated operations: upserts (valid, nil state, mismatched state ID,
  unparseable ID), deletes of any ID and of held IDs, obsolete upserts and deletes at a revision drawn from 1 to the
  held one, ticks, `BootstrapDone`.
- `TestHistoryOrderIrrelevant` draws one to ten entities (unparseable IDs included), one to four deliveries each at
  revisions 1..n, and after any upsert, an obsolete upsert or delete at a revision drawn from 1 to that upsert's. It
  runs two independently drawn interleavings with ticks at drawn points and compares the final buckets byte for byte
  and the final `Tick` results.
