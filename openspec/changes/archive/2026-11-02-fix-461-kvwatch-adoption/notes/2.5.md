# Task 2.5: `graph/readiness` watchOnce stops through `kvwatch.Stop`

## Test

`TestWatcherRunWaitsForABackedUpDeliveryAfterContextEnds` (`graph/readiness/watcher_test.go`), one `synctest.Test`
bubble, no subtests. A new scripted `backlogWatcher` stands in for nats.go v1.55.0's backed-up watch
(`internal/kvwatch` doc): its 256-entry updates buffer is filled before `Run` starts, a delivery goroutine sends 1,000
more behind it, then waits for `Stop` and only then closes the channel, and closes `delivered`. `Stop` leaves the
channel open. The scripted `fakeBucket.watch` waits on `<-ctx.Done()` before it returns the watcher, so `Run` never
reads the watch while ctx is live. The test waits (`synctest.Wait`) until `Run` is blocked in `Watch`, cancels, and
after `Run` returns checks:

- `Run` returned `context.Canceled`;
- `delivered` is closed (the delivery has ended): the expected assertion, `watcher_test.go:839`;
- the watch was stopped.

The existing `fakeWatcher` and `watchSource` stay as they are.

This scripted watcher proves only that `Run` stops through the drain and waits for it. That nats.go needs the drain
rests on `internal/kvwatch`'s own real-server evidence (`graph/follower`'s `TestFollowerRunReturnsAndLeavesNothing`),
not on this test.

## Choices the task left open

- **Explicit assertion in place of synctest's deadlock report.** The task says synctest reports the blocked goroutine
  on base. The test instead asserts on `delivered` right after `Run` returns, and on failure stops the watcher and
  reads the rest of the channel so the bubble ends with a named failure line rather than a deadlock panic. This also
  gives `task mutate:check` a location to expect. Without that branch the base would still fail, through synctest.
- **The delivery closes the channel only after `Stop`.** nats.go closes `Updates` after the watch is unsubscribed;
  the scripted goroutine waits on `stopped` before its close to keep that order.
- **Context passed to `kvwatch.Stop`.** `watchOnce`'s own `ctx` (`Run`'s), already ended on this path. The drain is
  bounded by `kvwatch.DrainBudget`, counted from when the watcher's own `Stop` returns; ctx's cancellation does not
  cut it short.
- **Log.** The stop error stays at Debug, "readiness watch stop", as before (proposal, "What Changes").
- **Docs.** `Run` now says it stops the watch and waits for its delivery to close, for at most 5 s after the watcher's
  own `Stop` returns, whether or not ctx has ended, and past that returns with the delivery still open; it points at
  `internal/kvwatch`'s doc for why nats.go needs the wait and makes no nats.go claim of its own. `watchOnce`
  (unexported) names `kvwatch.DrainBudget`. No exported signature changes or names `internal/kvwatch`.
- **Existing tests.** Their `fakeWatcher` never closes its channel, so every other test's `Run` now waits out the 5 s
  drain on the bubble's clock, logged at Debug as `kvwatch.ErrDeliveryOpen`. No real time:
  `go test -race -count=1 ./graph/readiness/` reads 1.305s after the change.
- **Ledger.** The `internal/kvwatch` dependency in the `graph/readiness` ledger row is task 2.6, not done here.
