# Task 4.1: `PrefixQueryResponse.Total`

Choices design D5 leaves open.

- **Where `total` is taken.** After the sort, before the cursor, in `handleQueryPrefixWithMaxPayload`: the one line every
  path reaches, after the exact-ID fallback has set its one key. It is `len(keys)`; no dedup and no new mechanism (#512).
- **Key order.** `entities`, `total`, `next_cursor`, so the empty group reads as the spec's literal,
  `{"entities":null,"total":0}`.
- **The fit.** `marshalFittingPrefixPage` takes `total` and measures its real width. A total of 12345 in the two
  existing fit tests is what shows a width of one digit is wrong; they stay exact.
- **The test's reply oracle.** A non-empty page carries timestamps and an opaque cursor, so `TestPrefixPageNamesItsTotal`
  cuts entities to IDs and the cursor to `true`, keeps every key the reply sent, and compares with literal JSON. An empty
  group's reply is compared whole, byte for byte.
- **A real byte-trimmed page.** Three entities of two fifths of `max_payload` each, requested with no limit: two fit, so
  the fit trims rather than the limit.
- **An exact ID that is absent** answers `{"entities":null,"total":0}` too; the test holds it beside the two empty groups.
- **The graph-ingest README** gains a sentence on `total`; the task names only the ledger and MIGRATION.

Generated checks: none. D5 records one law on literal seeds with no interacting inputs, so examples suffice. Fuzzing:
none; the change adds a field and parses nothing new.
