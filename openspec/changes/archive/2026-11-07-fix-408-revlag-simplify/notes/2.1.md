## Choices the design left open

- **In-flight structure (D3, owner ruling 1).** `container/list`, `PushBack` only, and
  `byKey map[string][]*list.Element` holding each key's elements in ascending order. The `pending` map is gone. Because
  a key's elements ascend, `Complete` drains a prefix of the key's slice and stops at the first revision above `rev`.
  `slices.Delete` removes that prefix: it shifts the kept elements and clears the vacated tail, so the backing array
  holds no drained element (claim 4). Its cost is the key's in-flight count (claim 2).
- **The record per revision** holds the revision and commit time only. The key is not stored in the list element,
  because `Complete` reaches elements through the key map.
- **The refusal** is a plain `fmt.Errorf` naming the revision, the key and `Observed`. There is no sentinel: the
  follower declares the feed lost on any error (task 2.2), and no caller distinguishes kinds.
- **`IndexedAt`** reads the front of the list directly. There is no separate floor helper, since `Observe` and
  `Complete` no longer need one.
- **The follower call site** (`graph/follower/follower.go:264`) calls `Observe` without reading its error, with a
  comment that task 2.2 declares the feed lost on a refusal. This keeps the tree building until 2.2 replaces it.
- **Test renames.** The time rule changed, so tests whose names stated the old rule were renamed:
  `MidBuildTakesNewestCovered` became `MidBuildTakesOldestInFlight`, `ObserveDoesNotRegressCoveredTime` became
  `NewDeliveryAfterCatchUp`, `DrainAtFloorIsCovered` became `DrainAtFloor` and `ObservedIsHighestDelivered` became
  `ObservedIsHighestAccepted`. `RevisionZeroIgnored` was folded into
  `TestWatermark_ObserveRefusesNonAscending/zero`. `TestWatermark_ZeroCommitTimeStaysUnknown` gained the spec scenario
  "An unknown oldest time". `TestWatermark_TimeIsOldestInFlight` gained a case where commit times fall as revisions
  rise (revision 1 at 9 s, revision 2 at 3 s; want 9 s). It separates "by revision" from "by time".
- **Checkpoints (D4).** The test asks the reader for a read over a channel, and the reader replies with a fresh
  `IndexedAt` and `Observed` taken after the ask. A phase `sync.WaitGroup`, which each worker marks done after its
  `Complete`, orders both checkpoints after the writes. The `observedB` channel orders checkpoint B after
  `Observe(2000)`.
- **The model's commit times** for the `next` op are drawn apart from the revision (0 to 10 s), so the model checks the
  time rule by revision, not by time. A `zero` op (`Observe(0, …)`) joins `lower` and `reobserve`.

size, not measured. `IndexedAt` costs the same at both sizes.
