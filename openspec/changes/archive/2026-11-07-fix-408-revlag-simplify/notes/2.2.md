## Choices the design left open

- **How the test drives `follow`.** The test builds a `Follower` struct literal in the package, with only the fields
  `follow`, `Stuck` and `QueryReady` read: `logger`, `deliver`, `failed`, `reader` and `watermark`. No production seam
  was added. The fake `kvcatalog.CatalogReader` embeds the interface and implements only `WatchAll`. Its watcher's
  buffered channel holds the snapshot marker, revision 5 and revision 3. The marker comes first, so both writes are
  live and no stored entity is validated (a validation failure would latch a reset, which `QueryReady` reports first).
- **Ordering.** `follow` runs on the test goroutine. `Deliver` for revision 3 cancels the context, so `follow` stops
  the watch (the fake's `Stop` closes the channel) and returns. Its return orders the assertions after both
  deliveries. The test has no sleep and no deadline of its own.
- **Closing the channel would hide the defect.** A watch whose channel closes declares the feed lost by itself
  ("the watch closed"), so the test ends `follow` through the context instead, which declares nothing.
- **`Stuck` is checked with `t.Fatalf`.** Without a lost feed, `QueryReady` goes on to read the stream status, which
  the fake does not implement, and would panic. The fatal keeps the base failure a plain assertion. `QueryReady` is
  checked for class transient, code `index_not_ready` and the text "feed is lost", so the incomplete-build refusal
  cannot pass for it.
- **The test also checks the entry is delivered** (D2): delivered revisions are `[5 3]`.
- **Generated checks.** Not used. The follower adds one branch on `Observe`'s error. Which revisions `Observe`
  refuses is the watermark's rule, checked by `TestPropWatermarkHistory` and
  `TestWatermark_ObserveRefusesNonAscending` (task 2.1). One out-of-order delivery is enough to show the branch.
- **The lost-feed cause** reads "the watch delivered a revision out of order: " plus the watermark's error, which
  names the revision, the key and `Observed`.
- **Doc comments beyond the list in D8.** The package doc's lost-feed limit now names an out-of-order delivery as a
  trigger. In `pkg/graphview/view.go` the `Applied` comment said its time "mirrors"
  `IndexStatusInputs.IndexedAt`. That stopped being true: `Applied` is the newest applied write, and the field is
  the oldest unapplied one. The comment now says the two differ and names the field. `index_status.go:106-108` now
  says the age is exact for delivered writes, as D1 states.
- **The rename** was done with `gopls rename` under `GOFLAGS=-tags=integration`. Before it, `gopls references`
  found the field at `graph/follower/follower.go:426`, `graph/readiness/index_status.go:72` and six lines of
  `graph/readiness/index_status_test.go`. The local `indexedAt` in `compute` keeps its name, because it holds what
  `Watermark.IndexedAt` returns. Archived changes still name the old field; they are history and were not edited.
- **PR #544.** `origin/main` was not merged in this task, as the brief asked for no push or merge. Whether #544 has
  landed was not checked.
