## Implementation

- `processor/graph-embedding/component.go`: `cleanup(ctx)` initiates all subscription drains, cancels `runCancel`, waits
  up to `ctx` on `feeds`/`repairs`/`worked`/`cached`, and initiates `coalescer.Shutdown(ctx)` without returning early on
  a prior `ctx.Err()`.
- `processor/graph-index/component.go`: `cleanup(ctx)` initiates all subscription drains, cancels `runCancel`, waits up
  to `ctx` on `feeds`, and initiates `pool.Shutdown(ctx)` without returning early, keeping `poolCtx` live until accepted
  work finishes writing on retry.
- `processor/graph-query/component.go`: `cleanup(ctx)` initiates all subscription drains, cancels `runCancel`, and waits
  up to `ctx` on `supervised` without returning early, reporting `supervisorErr` once all handles join.
- `output/websocket/websocket.go`: `cleanup(ctx)` fences admissions, closes `server`, initiates `sub.Drain(ctx)`, closes
  all connected clients (`1001`), closes `listen`, waits up to `ctx` on `served` and `requests.Wait(ctx)`, cancels
  `runCancel`, and waits up to `ctx` on `supervised` without returning early.
