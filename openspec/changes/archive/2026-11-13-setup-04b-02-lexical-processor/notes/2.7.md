## Choices the design leaves open

1. **Which words.** D8 names "a word one fixed name holds". The check asks every entity's own word (`keywords`, the
   function graph-embedding's check already uses: the last word of its name no other name holds), live and deleted,
   so the one-name word and the deleted entity's word are the same rule over every entity. `field` is the word every
   base's name holds.
2. **Scores are checked, not only hit sets.** Each one-word reply's hits all score the same, above 0: a keyword has
   one hit, and every base's name is two words with `field` one of them, so the four bases tie and order by ID. The
   expected order is written from that, not read from a reply.
3. **No `min_revision`.** The request carries none; the wait asks again until the reply's own `indexed_revision`
   reaches the revision, as D8 says. A reply that answers at all is then checked, so an early reply cannot pass.
4. **Refusals during the wait.** `index_not_ready` is asked again unless its message says the feed is lost, which
   holds until the process restarts; every other refusal ends the wait and fails. Matching on the message mirrors
   `checkSearch`'s handling of graph-embedding's lost cache watch.
5. **What a restart comparison covers.** The words and replies the first process gave (`checkAnswers` returns them)
   are asked again of the restarted process, waiting on `indexed_revision` at the revision read before the stop,
   before any new write, and compared whole (`reflect.DeepEqual` on the reply's data: hits, order, scores, matched,
   bodies not indexed). The envelope's `indexed_revision` and `timestamp` are not compared: they are not the answer.
6. **Two restart tests, not three.** `TestSmoke_StackRestartKeepsAnswers` (the spec names it) and
   `TestSmoke_BrokerRestartBetweenRuns` compare; both have a moment after the restart and before any write.
   `TestSmoke_BrokerRestartUnderRunningStack` keeps one process, so it has no rebuild to compare; it checks the word
   search after its writes and delete, through `checkAnswers`. `TestSmoke_KillMidIngestKeepsAcknowledgedWrites` has
   no answers from the killed process to compare with.
7. **`ask` became `askResponder` underneath** so the lexical verb's subject is read from the table by responder; `ask`
   keeps its graph-query callers unchanged.
