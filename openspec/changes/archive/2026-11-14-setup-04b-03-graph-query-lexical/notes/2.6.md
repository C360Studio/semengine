# Task 2.6 notes: smoke on the word index (PR B)

Base commit `ed018274`.

## What changed

`internal/smoke/smoke_integration_test.go`:

- **One oracle for every word search.** `wordHits` writes each word's expected hits from the fixed names (each
  entity's own keyword answers it alone while live and nothing once deleted; `field` answers exactly the live bases,
  ordered by ID). `checkLexical` (graph-lexical's verb) and the new `checkWordSearches` (graph-query's two verbs) both
  read it, so graph-query's answers are held to the names, not only to graph-lexical's reply.
- **Every word, both verbs, deleted words included.** `checkCommunities` now asks `localSearch` on the lead and
  `globalSearch` on every word of `wordHits`, and asserts the matches in order, `matched`, the group and level, no
  `missing`, not `truncated`, and `indexed_revision` at or above the revision. Global search names the lead's group
  with its match count, `record_state` `read` and the record's member count when anything matches, and no group when
  nothing does. This adds the spec delta's "a deleted entity's word answers nothing from `localSearch` or global
  search", which was not checked.
- **Waits.** `askSearch` asks a graph-query search with `min_revision` until it is not the word index catching up
  (`wordIndexCatchingUp`: `revision_not_reached`, or `index_not_ready` that is not a lost feed). graph-query passes
  `min_revision` to graph-lexical, so an answer is read from a word index whose `indexed_revision` reached it; no wall
  clock. The record wait (localSearch until the record holds all members) stays, as graph-query's community view has
  no revision a test can wait on.
- The bm25 assertions (`checkSearch`, `EmbedderType == "bm25"`) are left for change 4.

## Generated checks or examples

The fixture is fixed and every word's answer is written out by `wordHits`; named examples cover a live keyword, a
deleted keyword and a word several entities share. No law over generated inputs is claimed.
