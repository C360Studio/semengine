## Choices the task leaves open

- **No test moved.** Every test in `bm25_embedder_test.go` checked the hashed embedder's own arithmetic
  (tokenizing, stop words, stemming, normalization, term statistics, `GenerateQuery` not learning, nil-context
  refusal). None proved a behaviour of the Worker, Storage, the cache or graph-embedding; those are tested through
  the stub embedders already in the package and, for graph-embedding, through 2.4's `wordEmbedder`.
- **Test data renamed.** Tests that used `"bm25"` and `"bm25-384"` as an embedder type or model name now use
  `"word-count"` and `"word-count-384"` (`graph/embedding` dedup, storage, offloaded, truncation and worker tests,
  `graph/query_search_types_test.go`, `processor/graph-query/revision_integration_test.go`). They are only labels;
  the assertions are unchanged.
- **`MaxTextLen` comment.** `dedup.go` said the cap was derived from the embedder type (4,000 for bm25). It is
  graph-embedding's `max_text_len` now, so the comment says the cap is configured apart from the embedder.
- **Ledger.** The `graph/embedding` row lists `bm25_embedder.go` and its test under "Not carried", drops the test
  from `proving_tests` and the "BM25 is a lexical embedder" risk, and its purpose names the product's embedder.
  The `graph/query` row's "built the hashed BM25 embedder" stays: it records why the example classifier went.
