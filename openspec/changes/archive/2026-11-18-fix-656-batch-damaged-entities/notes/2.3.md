## Choices the design leaves open

- `readMatches` returns an unexported `readGaps{missing int; damaged []string}` in place of the count: the design
  says it returns the damaged IDs beside the count, and a struct keeps the two call sites from misbinding two
  positional results. `readGaps.left` is the one place that says a gap sets `truncated`.
- Each test names two damaged hits, listed by the batch in the reverse of hit order, so the reply's order is shown to
  come from the hits, not from the batch reply. The design's example has one; two is the smallest that tests order.
- `TestSearchNamesDamagedHit` asks globalSearch a two-word question. A one-word question is read as a reference and
  asks the alias verb, which no stub serves; that hint and its counted error are not what the test is about.
- In the merged test the damaged hits are found by meaning alone, as the spec scenario says. The lists are chosen so
  no two merged scores tie, and both fit the limit, so `truncated` can come only from the damaged hits.
- Generated checks: not used. The rule has two inputs per hit (returned, or named with a reason) and no history; the
  named examples cover returned, damaged and, in `TestSearchDropsMissingHits`, `not_found`.

- A batch that names a hit both returned and damaged: the returned entity wins, as it did for any missing reason.
