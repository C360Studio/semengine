## Choices the design leaves open

- The report is made by the component, after `applier.deliver` refuses and before `applier.removeDamaged` deletes the
  text, so a search never misses the text uncounted. `removeDamaged` shares the delete path (`drop`): the key keeps
  the damaged revision, so an older delivery of it is ignored, as after a delete.
- Scope: an unscoped search counts every damaged entity (nil filter, so a key that is not an entity ID counts too); a
  scoped one counts those `graph.MatchesAnyIDPrefix` keeps, the rule the search itself applies.
- The count is the larger of the record read before and after the search, as graph-index does (`5.1.md`).
