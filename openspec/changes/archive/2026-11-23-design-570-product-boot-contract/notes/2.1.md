# 2.1: the platform refusal test, shown failing

Test: `TestComponentManagerRefusesAPlatformOtherThanTheEstablishedOne` in
`service/component_manager_platform_integration_test.go`.

It starts a `config.Manager` declaring org `o` and stem `s` on a freshly reset server, reads the minted pair from
`GetConfig().Get().Platform`, and runs `ConfigureFromServices` once per case over a fresh component registry whose
one factory counts its calls. The two fields of `deps.Platform` each take three values (empty, the effective value,
something else), so the nine subtests are every combination; no generated check is needed.

The oracle for "accepted" is written from the requirement, not from the code: the pair is wholly empty, or both
fields equal the effective `org` and `id`.
