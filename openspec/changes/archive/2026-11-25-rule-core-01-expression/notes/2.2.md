## Review fix: an unprepared regex on an absent field

Codex review of PR #753 at `71bef230`: the compiled-pattern check ran only after the field was found, so an
uncompiled, JSON-copied or edited regex condition on an absent optional field returned false, and under `or` with
a true condition the expression passed.

`TestUnpreparedRegexRefusedWhenFieldAbsent` (`compile_test.go`) was written first: absent bare (`a.b.missing`),
`$state.missing` and `$message.missing` fields, each uncompiled, JSON-copied and edited, under `and` alone and under
`or` with a true condition; a compiled optional regex on an absent field stays false with no error. Before the fix:

```text
--- FAIL: TestUnpreparedRegexRefusedWhenFieldAbsent/a.b.missing/json_copy (0.00s)
--- FAIL: TestUnpreparedRegexRefusedWhenFieldAbsent/a.b.missing/edited (0.00s)

--- FAIL: TestUnpreparedRegexRefusedWhenFieldAbsent/$state.missing/uncompiled (0.00s)
```

The fix: `checkCompiled` (`regex.go`) runs first in `evaluateConditionWithStateAndMessage`, before any field is
