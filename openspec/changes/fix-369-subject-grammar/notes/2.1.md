## Choices the design does not spell out

- The explicit-stream site also carries a `config_invalid` finding through `Validate` (the test streams declare no
  bounds). The design expects this ("some cases may also carry `config_invalid`"); T1 counts `malformed_subject` only.
- The NATS-request ports are named `ask` (input) and `reply` (output) so their messages differ from the NATS sites.
- Each malformed message is asserted as literal text: the site's prefix written by hand plus the subject quoted by
  hand (`quoted` in the test), never built with `%q`.
- Suggestions are asserted non-empty, as `TestValidateRefusesStreamCapturingReservedSubject` does, not word for word.
