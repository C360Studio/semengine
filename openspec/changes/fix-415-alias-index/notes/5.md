## Removed, and why

- `TestUnsafeAliasSkipIsLoggedAndCounted` (`index_integration_test.go`) pinned the KV-unsafe skip, its warning and
  `aliases_skipped_total`; all three go (ADR-114 §5). `TestKVUnsafeAliasIsIndexed` replaces it: same entity, same
  other-row assertions, plus a whitespace-only alias that must get no row and a read of `two words`.
- `TestQueryRefusesInvalidRequestsBeforeAnyRead` (`query_test.go`): its alias cases `two words`, `alpha.>` and
  `alpha.*` pinned the read's `ValidateKVLiteralKey` refusal and, once valid, reached the bucket read of an unstarted
  component (nil-pointer panic). They are valid aliases now; replaced by `   `, the one refusal the read keeps.

- `updates_total{index_type="alias"}` was already counted once per entity in the success loops (`index.go`, the
  `[]string{"name", "alias", …}` lists); nothing to move.
- Not in this task: `processor/graph-index/README.md:46` and `:174` and `docs/admission-ledger.yaml:3434` still name
