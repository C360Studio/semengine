## Choices the design leaves open

- Type `int`, as the search replies' other counts (`Matched`, `BodiesNotIndexed`).
- `NewQueryResponse` keeps its signature; a producer sets the field on the value it built. Every producer would
  otherwise pass a 0 it never chose until PR 3, and the constructor's job (refusing an empty producer, stamping the
  time) does not touch the count.
- The field sits after `IndexedRevision`; JSON key order does not matter to any decoder.
