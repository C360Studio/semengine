## Choices the design leaves open

- **One unexported type, `indexRead`** (`processor/graph-query/query.go`), carries the revision and the count
  together where a `uint64` revision travelled before: `resolveReference`, `outgoingRows`, `incomingRows`,
  `predicateRows`, `pathHop`, `walkPaths`, `neighbours`, `envelope`. Its `and` method is the one place the two
  rules meet: smallest revision, largest count. A separate `int` return would have made three non-error returns.
- **Counts are not added.** Two reads (graph-lexical and graph-embedding, or two hops) may count the same damaged
  entity, so the largest is what the reply is known to lack. This is the design's "largest".
- **A verb that reads one index reply** (`relationships`, `entityByAlias`, `summary`, `localSearch`) carries that
  reply's count; the test stubs it at 3. The "2 and 3 give 3" cases run on the verbs that read two or more:
  `pathSearch` (two hops, both orders), `globalSearch` (word search and the alias read of an entity hint, both
  orders) and `searchGraph` at tier 1 (word and embedding searches, both orders). Each also runs at 0 and 0, which
  must leave the key out.
- **Global search counts its hint reads.** The alias read, and the walk of a path hint, put an entity or a path in
  the reply, so their counts join the word search's. A hint not honoured puts nothing in the reply, so its reads
  count nothing (`hinted`). The revision rule is unchanged: global search keeps the word search's revision.
- **searchGraph at tier 1** counts the embedding reply only when its list was used (`semanticRead.damaged`, set only
  on an honoured read); a failed merge answers from the word list alone and carries only the word search's count.
  Answered from the embedding search alone (word index not ready), the reply carries the embedding count under
  revision 0.
- **summary with predicates left out** reads no index reply, so it carries no count, as it carries revision 0.
- `TestPathSearchMatchesTheModel`'s generated walk now also checks the largest count: each hop's count is its drawn
  revision modulo 5, and the test computes the largest itself.
- The test envelope type `wireEnvelope` (`composed_integration_test.go`) gained the key, since it decodes strictly.
