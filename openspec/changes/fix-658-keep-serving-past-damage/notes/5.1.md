## Choices the design leaves open

- A key that is not a literal entity ID is recorded with a fatal `graph_state_reset_required` refusal, reason
  `noncanonical_entity_id`, at the delivered revision, and completes. No row can exist under it (every write path
  validates the key first), so nothing is removed. Before, its revision stayed pending and it was marked failed.
- A value naming another entity than its key is damaged like any refused value: B's own rows are removed (the filters
  are B's), and the revision completes. `errAuthoritativeIdentityMismatch` lost its only use and is gone.
- The revision reported is the one `Get` read (`entry.Revision()`), as D1 says; a repair pass reads it too.
- A failed removal is marked failed by `deleteFromIndexes` itself, as for a delete, so the repair lane retries it; it
  counts its own error beside the damage's.
- The count in a reply is the larger of the record read with the floor (before the handler's first bucket read) and
  the record read when the reply is built. One read after the bucket reads would miss an entity whose repair wrote
  its rows after the read and cleared it before the count; the larger of the two misses it only when the entity is
  damaged and repaired again within one reply.
- `outgoing` checks the recorded refusal after reading the row, in both the found and not-found branches, so an
  entity recorded while the row was read is refused rather than answered empty.
