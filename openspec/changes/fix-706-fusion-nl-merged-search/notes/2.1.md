## The 4 s bound waits 4 real seconds

`TestMergedSearchEmbeddingWaitBound` waits the real 4 s. The embedding request's deadline is the timeout
`natsclient.Client.RequestClassified` takes, and graph-query holds a concrete `*natsclient.Client`, so no seam the
code already accepts avoids it; `testing/synctest` does not advance its clock while a goroutine waits on a network
read, and a clock or client hook on the component would be a back door. The test asks with timeout 0, natsclient's
default 5 s: the caller budget the bound is chosen to fit, not a deadline of the test's own. With
`query_timeout_ms` 15000 and no bound the call times out, as the mutation row above shows.

## Oracle

`TestMergedSearchMatchesSearchGraphOrder` takes searchGraph's reply as the oracle for IDs and scores, at tier 1 and
tier 0, limits 10 and 100, after checking searchGraph left no hit missing. `TestMergedSearchReplyShape` and
`TestMergedSearchWordIndexNotReady` check scores worked out by hand from 1 / (60 + place). Examples suffice over
generated checks: the merge law itself is `TestMergeRanks`'s, generated against a reference model; these tests pin
the verb's wiring around it.
