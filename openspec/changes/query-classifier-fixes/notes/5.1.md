## Choices the design leaves open

- **Triggers.** A trigger is a match of a pattern the keyword tier reads for the operation: count `countPattern`;
  avg `avgPattern`; sum `sumPattern` except the "total" of "total number of" (a count trigger, so "Do not give the
  total number of sensors" cannot fall through to sum with field `number`; row "negated total number of is no sum");
  min and max `minPattern`, `maxPattern` and the D1 numeric extremum phrases the keyword tier would read (an extremum
  after which/who/with in its clause gives no hint, so it is no trigger); similarity `similarityPattern`; ranking
  `topNPattern`, `bottomNPattern`, `mostPattern`, `leastPattern`.
- **One excluded trigger excludes its operation**, even when another trigger of the same operation is affirmative
  ("Do not count sensors; count pumps" gives no count). Fail closed; D2 says an operation is excluded when "its
  trigger" is.
- **"or" rule.** In the clause holding an `or`, if a trigger lies before it and one after it, and they name at least
  two different operations, every operation among them is excluded. "count sensors or count pumps" keeps count.
  Limit: commas split clauses, so "count, average or sum" excludes avg and sum but keeps count.
- **Negating words.** `don't` is matched with a straight or a typographic apostrophe (`don’t`), as phones type it.
  "do not" is matched by its `not`. Word boundaries keep "Annotate" and "nothing" from negating.
- **Quotes.** Straight quotes pair in order; a curly `“` pairs with the next `”`; an unpaired mark opens nothing
  (row "unbalanced quote"). A trigger is inside when its first byte is.
- **Clause breaks read on the whole question.** `clauseBefore` used to match breaks in `query[:pos]`, where `$`
  matched at `pos`, so a mark directly before a trigger ("not.count") ended a clause, against notes/4.1.md's rule
  that a mark with no space after it ends nothing. `clauseStart` now matches breaks on the whole question (row "no
  space after a mark is one clause"). Section 4's rows are unchanged.
- **Other hints stand.** "Do not count sensors from yesterday" keeps its `time_range` at tier 0
  (`TestKeywordExclusionKeepsOtherHints`), as D2's table puts "any hint" first.
- The examples in `TestChainKeepsExclusions` are the pin's iot shapes (`configs/domains/iot.json` at `8b99efe9`):
  three pin examples plus D2's "Count devices in error state" and "Find devices similar to TEMP-001". Each question in
  part (2) gets its own example classifier, so no earlier question changes its scores.
- Generated checks: `FuzzKeywordClassify` (task 5.2) for the two laws; the clause, negation, quote and `or` rules are
  each shown both ways by named rows.

## Review fix (in-house review of 8403f0f): the "or" rule

The "or" rule excluded any operation before an "or" together with any different operation after it in the clause,
even when the "or" joined nouns. New rows in `TestKeywordExclusions`, run at `8403f0f` before the fix:

```text

    --- FAIL: TestKeywordExclusions/or_joins_nouns (0.00s)
    --- FAIL: TestKeywordExclusions/or_joins_adjectives (0.00s)
    --- FAIL: TestKeywordExclusions/or_joins_verbs (0.00s)
    --- FAIL: TestKeywordExclusions/likely_is_no_similarity (0.00s)
```

- The rule now fires only when the second operation's trigger starts right after the "or", with an optional "the"
  or "an" between them. A trigger of a different operation before the "or", in the same clause, completes the pair.
  "How many sensors or pumps have an average temperature above 30?" gives count; "Is the maximum pressure higher or
  lower than the average?" gives avg; "Count sensors that failed or are likely to" gives count. R31, "the minimum or
  maximum pressure", "count sensors or the average pressure" and "count sensors or an average pressure" stay
  excluded.
- "likely" matched the inherited similarity pattern `\b(similar|like|resembl|compar)\w*`. `like` is now a whole word
  (`like\b`); the other three keep their endings. Row "Show sensors likely to fail" gives the default result. Cost:
  "likes", "liked", "likewise" no longer give similarity.

## Codex findings at b37aaa6: "or a" before the second operation

Codex's review of `b37aaa6` found that `orPattern` skipped an optional "the" or "an" after "or" but not "a", so in
"Maybe average pressure or a count of sensors; I have not decided" the count trigger did not start where the "or"
match ended, both operations stayed eligible, and count won by precedence. New rows in `TestKeywordExclusions`, run
with `graph/query/classifier.go` unchanged from `b37aaa6`:

```text
go test -count=1 -v -run 'TestKeywordExclusions' ./graph/query
    actual  : &query.ClassificationResult{Tier:0, Intent:"",
              Options:map[string]interface {}{"aggregation_type":"count"}, Confidence:1}
    Messages: "Maybe average pressure or a count of sensors; I have not decided" activates nothing
--- FAIL: TestKeywordExclusions (0.00s)
    --- FAIL: TestKeywordExclusions/or_a_before_the_second (0.00s)
```

The other two new rows passed, as they must: "or an, count first" ("Maybe count sensors or an average pressure; I
have not decided", the default result) and "or a joins nouns" ("Count sensors with a pump or a valve", count).

- **Fix.** `orPattern` is `\bor\s+(?:(?:the|an|a)\s+)?`: "a" joins "the" and "an" in the one optional-article
  position. The rows where "or" joins nouns, adjectives or verbs are unchanged and pass.
- **Mutation check** (`task mutate:check`): `orPattern` without `a`, `TestKeywordExclusions`, expected text "Maybe
  average pressure or a count": detection.
