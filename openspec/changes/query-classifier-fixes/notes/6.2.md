# Task 6.2 notes

## Choices the design leaves open

- **The seam.** `KeywordClassifier.classify(query, now)` returns `(*SearchOptions, excluded bool)`, as section 5
  added it; both `KeywordClassifier.ClassifyQuery` and `ClassifierChain.ClassifyQuery` call it with `time.Now()`.
  The bounds are checked by `numbersOutOfRange(query)`, which the chain calls before any tier and turns into
  `errs.WrapInvalid(err, "ClassifierChain", "ClassifyQuery", "read the question's numbers")` (Codex finding at
  `b37aaa6`, `notes/6.1.md`; until then `classify` returned the error and the chain checked only with a keyword
  tier). The range helpers take
  `now` as an argument; `getLastNHoursRange` is gone (the checked span replaces it).
- **The error names the phrase as matched and the bound**, for example
  `"last 3000000 hours": the span must fit a time.Duration, at most 2562047 hours` and
  `"within 5km of 95,10": the latitude must be in [-90, 90]`. A question with a bad time phrase and a bad place
  phrase gets both, joined (`errors.Join`).
- **Every phrase is checked** (review finding, after `5beb454`): every "last N hours", "last N days" and "within"
  phrase in the question, not only the one that supplies the hint. Any one past its bound refuses the question at
  the chain. The keyword tier still takes its hint from the same phrase as before and keeps it when that phrase is
  valid.
- **A refused "last N" phrase stops the temporal search** when it is the first match: no later pattern ("last
  week", "this month") is tried for that question, so the refusal is never replaced by a different range.
- **A number past `int64` or `float64`** ("last 99999999999999999999 hours", a 401-digit radius) is out of range,
  not a parse failure: the patterns admit only ASCII digits (and, for "last N", an optional `-`), so a parse error
  can only mean overflow. Main dropped the first silently. A negative N is below 1 (Codex finding at `b37aaa6`).
- **A zero radius** ("within 0km of 1,2") still gives no box and no error, as at the pin; D6 bounds R only by being
  finite. Coordinates are checked first, so "within 0km of 95,10" is refused for its latitude.
- **Pole rule.** A box reaches a pole when its North is at least 90 or its South at most -90 before clamping; it
  then spans West -180 to East 180. A box that does not reach a pole has a longitude half-width of at most 180
  degrees, since 180·cos(lat) ≥ 90 − |lat| for every latitude, so only the antimeridian case leaves [-180, 180].
- **Ended context first.** A nil or ended context still gives `nil, nil` before the keyword tier runs, as D6 says.

## "This month" ends one nanosecond before the next

The developer found that `getThisMonthRange` ended the month at `nextMonth.Add(-time.Second)` (23:59:59.000, as at the
pin), cutting off the last second, while "today", "this week" and "this year" end at .999999999. It is one line in the
time ranges this section owns, so it is fixed here rather than filed: `nextMonth.Add(-time.Nanosecond)`. The three
"this month" rows of `TestTimeRangesFromOneInstant` now expect .999999999; with the old line restored they fail
(checked by hand, then the fix put back).
