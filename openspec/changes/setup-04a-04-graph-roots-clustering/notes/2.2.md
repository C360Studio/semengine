maintainer's D5 tests (`TestObsoleteDeliveryIgnored`, `TestRefusedEntityMakesTickIncomplete`,

## Generated checks or examples

Named examples suffice for these files: word extraction is a single-pass scan whose interacting cases are the length
bounds (3, 32, 33) and the separators, each a named case; the record bound is one worst case built at every field's
maximum. The order-dependent history is the maintainer's, covered by `TestMaintainerMatchesModel` (task 2.3).
