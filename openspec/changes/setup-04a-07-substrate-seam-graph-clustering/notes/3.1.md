## Choices the design leaves open

- **`detection_interval` is `1s` in the stack's configuration.** At the 30 s default every community wait would last
  up to 30 s, five tests over. A product may set the interval; the smoke configuration says why in its comment.
- **What the community wait reads.** graph-clustering's `GRAPH_STATUS` key counts what the maintainer holds (design
  D1), and graph-query answers from its own view of `COMMUNITY_INDEX`, so neither the key nor a bucket read orders a
  read of graph-query's answer. `checkCommunities` asks `localSearch` on the lead with `min_revision` set to the
  `ENTITY_STATES` revision `awaitIndexed` read after the last write; graph-query compares it with the record's
  `entity_revision` before answering (`processor/graph-query/localsearch.go`, `revision_not_reached`). So the wait
  is on the record's `entity_revision` as graph-query holds it. `index_not_ready` (no view yet: graph-query reopens
  a missing `COMMUNITY_INDEX` every 5 s) and `revision_not_reached` are asked again; any other refusal fails; a
  latched graph-clustering envelope, read beside each ask, fails at once naming it. The wait is under the test's
  context, so `go test -timeout` is its one deadline.
- **The text question is asked once, after the `localSearch` answer**, with the same `min_revision`: the view held a
  record at the revision then. It is `globalSearch` on the one word `field`; a one-word question naming no alias is
  searched as text (`globalEntity`), and the reply must say `strategy: text`.
- **`checkAnswers` takes the revision.** Every call site reads it from the `awaitIndexed` just before, so the waits
  stay after the writes, as the smoke tests already ordered them.
- **graph-clustering's key joins `fullStack.indexKeys`**, so `awaitIndexed` also waits on it and fails at once on a
  latched state (a lost feed, `reset_required`); the composed-stack spec names the key's revision. The no-embedder
  stack is unchanged: three components, no community records (its spec scenario is not this change's).

## The oracle

Expected values are written from the fixed inputs, never from a reply:

- the group is `c360.<platform>.fleet.ops` (level 0: org.platform.system.domain, ADR-102), its member count and the
  entities `localSearch` scanned are the live entities in `want`, nothing truncated;
- `localSearch`'s word is the lead's name's first word (`falcon`), and the text question's is `field`; for each, the
  matches are `want.matching(word)`: the live entities with the word inside a predicate or a string value, the score
  rule `graph.LocalSearchData` and the text search state, sorted by ID (the words are no type, so all matches tie). In
  the kill test this includes the held drone, `Gyrfalcon`;
- the text question's groups are exactly that one record with that member count, level 0, no unreadable group, and
  both replies' `indexed_revision` is at or above the revision.

Generated checks: not used. The inputs are the fixed entities the smoke tests already publish; the community checks
add two named questions over them, and the scoring rule they rest on has its own tests in graph-query.
