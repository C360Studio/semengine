## Scorecard (`TestKeywordsScorecard`)

The word index's frozen corpus (2,000 sections) as entities `c360.semengine.<group>.markdown.section.<n>`, the
level-0 group being the file's parent directory (263 groups; 46 of ten or more members are searched). "Common": a
keyword held by 20% or more of all sections. "Precision": a group's keywords searched on the word index, share of the
first ten hits inside the group, averaged over the 46.

| Rule | Common | Precision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most counted (before; failing-first run) | 27.9% | 66.7% |
| Distinctive, top-32 per member (first run) | 7.9% | 75.7% |
| Reference: plain TF-IDF over every section's words, in the test | 2.9% | 77.8% |

The before row reproduces the probe exactly. Floors: the first run given one point, `commonCeiling` 8.9% and
`precisionFloor` 74.7%, each better than before. The gap to the reference is the 32-word cap per member: a word
outside a member's top 32 is not counted as held, so a common word ranks higher than it would over every word.

## Choices the design leaves open

- **A zero or negative score is no keyword.** A word every member with words holds scores ln(1) = 0. The spec's
  scenario "a word common to the graph" needs it left out; kept as a zero-score candidate it would fill short lists.
  Side effect: a graph of one entity, or one whose members all hold the same words, has no keywords. Six example
  fixtures (`TestObsoleteDeliveryIgnored`, `TestUpsertDuringBlockedWrite`, `TestRecordNamesItsLatestWrite`,
  `TestDecodeCommunity`, `TestKeywordTextMatchesLexical`, and the integration `TestTextSuffixesIsRead`) gained an
  entity in another deployment holding other words; their assertions are on the original entity's groups and are
  unchanged in strength. `TestUpsertDuringBlockedWrite`'s words were chosen so the keywords still tell the final
  members apart (alpha needs n1 and n3; beta would need n2). `TestKeywordTextMatchesLexical` gained a twin member
  with the same values: in its group of two (with the untexted member) a word needs two holders.
- **graph-query's `recordJSON` follows the reply's `omitempty`**: a record with no keywords has none in the reply,
  which its literal had spelled as `"keywords": null`. `TestLocalSearchIsScopedToTheGroup`'s second member gained
  the word `station`, held by both members of its group and not by the entity outside it, so the attached record
  still carries a keyword and the test checks it does.
- **"Graph members with words"**, the spec delta's count, not every member: a member with no text holds nothing.
- **A failed tick requeues the groups it found unchanged too** (`maintainer.go`, `plan`). Before, a group whose
  rebuilt record equalled the one written was dropped from the pending set even when the tick then failed; the next
  good tick then kept a record scored against counts that had since moved, which the amended requirement ("the
  graph-wide counts of the last successful tick after the group last changed") forbids. Rapid found it
  (`upsert, size-only, bootstrap, failing-tick, failing-tick`); `TestFailedTickRebuildsUnchangedGroups` is the named
  witness. The extra cost is a rebuild, never a write, and only after a failed tick.
- **`topCounted` is generic over int and float64**, one top-n routine for types and scores.
- **The scorecard reads `graph/lexical/testdata/scorecard`**, as `processor/graph-query`'s scorecard does; the
  grouping function is the probe's.
- **Generated checks**: kept and extended (the rule is order-dependent history): the model's generator draws
  size-only revisions; both property tests compare after a restart.
