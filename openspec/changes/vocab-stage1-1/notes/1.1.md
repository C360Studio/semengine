# Developer Notes: vocab-stage1-1 (`#256`)

## 1. Implementation Choices

- **Single canonical `Validate() error` spelling**: `(s ProjectionSpec) Validate() error` and `(p *PackSpec) Validate()
  error` follow `(p PrefixSpec) Validate() error` (`vocabulary/pack.go:23`) without adding duplicate top-level wrapper
  functions, consistent with `#219`.
- **Pure `PackSpec.Validate()` isolation**: `(*PackSpec).Validate()` validates into local `packPredicates` and
  `packEntityTypes` maps without acquiring `registryMu` or reading `predicateRegistry` / `entityTypeRegistry`, and
  checks intra-pack duplicate prefix labels, predicate names, and entity-type keys as well as intra-pack `InverseOf`
  symmetry. `RegisterPack` calls `pack.Validate()` first, then stages into clones of `predicateRegistry` and
  `entityTypeRegistry` under `registryMu` so cross-pack inverse consistency and cross-pack amendments continue to work.
- **`export.SubjectIRI` and `normalizeBaseIRI`**: `export.SubjectIRI(subject, base)` trims trailing `'/'` characters on
  `base` (`strings.TrimRight(base, "/")`) and defaults an empty `base` to `vocabulary.SemEngineBase`. Both
  `subjectToIRI` and `resolvePredicateIRI` share `normalizeBaseIRI`.
- **Blank-node (`_:label`) and full-IRI predicate handling**: `isValidBlankNodeLabel` checks `strings.HasPrefix(s,
  "_:") && isValidPNLocal(s[2:])` (W3C Turtle / N-Triples `[141s] BLANK_NODE_LABEL`). Blank-node objects use a dedicated
  `objectBlankNode` kind in `classifiedObject` so `scanPrefixes` never attempts to prefix-compact a blank node.
  Unregistered `http://` and `https://` predicates that pass `isAbsoluteIRI` pass through `resolvePredicateIRI`
  unchanged after checking `vocabulary.GetPredicateMetadata(predicate)`.
